# malaysia-oil-shock-inflation
Analysis and forecasting of how Brent Crude oil shocks pass through to Malaysian inflation using official DOSM CPI data and scenario modelling

As the global oil prices roses sharply in March 2026, with Brent crude exceeding USD100 per barrel. Malaysia's fuel subsidies are expected to cushion the impact on comnsumers but it is still unclear how much of an oil shock passes through to inflation and how quickly it is. This project may helps policymakers, businesses and households anticipate cost pressures. This project will quantifies the pass-through from oil prices to Malaysian CPI and forecasts inflation under different oil price scenarios. 

Key Questions:
1. How much will the Transport CPI rise when the Brent rises, and after how many months?
2. Has pass-through changed since the 2024 diesel and 2025 RON95 subsidy changes?
3. What could Malaysian inflation look like if Brent reaches USD 80, 100 or 120?
